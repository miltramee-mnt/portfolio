Put your resume PDF in this folder and name it exactly:

    resume.pdf

The site links to it from three places:
  - the "View Resume" button in the navigation
  - the "View Resume" button in the hero
  - the "Download Resume" button in the Resume section

If you would rather link to a Google Drive / Dropbox file, just replace
the href="assets/docs/resume.pdf" values in index.html with your URL.
